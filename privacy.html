Privacy Policy

rclone EMBED Archive is a private, single-user archival utility operated by the owner of the associated Google account.

Google user data

The application uses Google Drive authorization only to access the owner's Google Drive for the purpose of storing, reading, and managing research archive files.

Data collection

The application does not collect personal information from third-party users and does not operate a public user database.

Data storage

Files transferred by the application are stored only in the owner's Google Drive account and on systems controlled by the owner as part of the archival workflow.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or transferred to unrelated third parties.

Purpose limitation

Google Drive access is used solely for the private archival and backup functions described on the application homepage.
